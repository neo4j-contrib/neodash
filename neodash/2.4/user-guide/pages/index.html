Pages

This documentation pertains to the (legacy) unsupported version of NeoDash. For users of the supported NeoDash offering, refer to NeoDash commercial.

A page is a collection of reports that can be viewed at the same time. Each page can have an unlimited number of reports in it, and will switch to a scrollable view when the number of reports do not fit in the user’s window.

An example of a dashboard with 4 pages can be seen below:

A dashboard may have as many pages as required by the dashboard builder. To switch pages, simply click on the page title in the dashboard header.

Editing Pages

Pages can be added, renamed, and deleted by using the buttons in the dashboard header (if editing is enabled). Pages can additionally be re-ordered by dragging and dropping them in the header.
